# Phase 1: Initial Diagnosis - MIAO-MB Implementation

## Overview
Phase 1 is the foundation of MIAO-MB implementation. This phase involves understanding the current state of biomedical maintenance operations, identifying gaps against ISO requirements, and building stakeholder engagement.

**Duration:** 2-3 weeks  
**Lead:** Project Sponsor / Quality Manager  
**Key Deliverables:** Diagnostic report, gap analysis, project charter

---

## 1. Project Initialization

### 1.1 Stakeholder Engagement

**Objective:** Build support and commitment for MIAO-MB implementation

**Activities:**
1. **Sponsor Briefing**
   - Meet with executive sponsor
   - Explain MIAO-MB benefits
   - Clarify timeline and resource needs
   - Secure commitment and support

2. **Project Team Identification**
   - Maintenance Director
   - Quality Manager
   - Lead Maintenance Technician
   - Representative from each department
   - IT support (if using software)

3. **Kickoff Meeting**
   - Explain purpose and objectives
   - Present timeline and milestones
   - Define roles and responsibilities
   - Address concerns
   - Build enthusiasm

4. **Communication Plan**
   - How will progress be communicated?
   - Frequency of updates
   - Distribution list
   - Method (email, meetings, bulletin board)

### 1.2 Project Documentation

**Create:**
- [ ] Project Charter
- [ ] Communication Plan
- [ ] Resource Plan

## 2. Current State Assessment

### 2.1 Document Collection

**Objective:** Gather all existing documentation to understand current practices

**Documents to Collect:**
- [ ] Existing maintenance procedures
- [ ] Equipment maintenance manuals
- [ ] Maintenance logs and records (past 12-24 months)
- [ ] Equipment purchase records
- [ ] Maintenance budget information
- [ ] Training records
- [ ] Any existing audit reports
- [ ] Incident reports
- [ ] Equipment failure data
- [ ] Spare parts inventory records

**Activities:**
1. Identify where documents are stored
2. Request documents from custodians
3. Organize documents by type
4. Scan important documents
5. Create document index

### 2.2 Equipment Inventory

**Objective:** Create comprehensive list of all biomedical equipment

**For Each Equipment, Document:**
- [ ] Equipment name and type
- [ ] Manufacturer and model
- [ ] Serial number
- [ ] Location/Department
- [ ] Installation date
- [ ] Purchase date
- [ ] Acquisition cost
- [ ] Annual maintenance cost
- [ ] Current condition (good/fair/poor)
- [ ] Maintenance history summary
- [ ] Critical/Essential/Important classification

**Data Collection Method:**
1. Physical walkthrough of facility
2. Interview department heads
3. Review equipment manuals
4. Check purchasing records
5. Document with photos if possible

**Output:** Equipment Inventory Spreadsheet with [Number of equipment] entries

### 2.3 Current Practice Assessment

**Objective:** Understand how maintenance is currently performed

**Interview Questions for Technicians:**
1. How do you decide when to perform maintenance?
2. What procedures do you follow?
3. How do you document your work?
4. What challenges do you face?
5. What works well?
6. What could be improved?
7. What training would help?

**Interview Questions for Department Users:**
1. Are you satisfied with equipment maintenance?
2. How often does equipment fail?
3. What is the impact of equipment downtime?
4. How do you report problems?
5. What would improve the service?

**Interview Questions for Management:**
1. What are your quality objectives?
2. What regulatory requirements apply?
3. What is the maintenance budget?
4. What are your main concerns?
5. What results do you want from MIAO-MB?

**Observation Activities:**
1. Observe maintenance being performed
2. Review how records are kept
3. Observe communication processes
4. Note any safety concerns
5. Identify efficiency issues

## 3. Compliance Gap Analysis

### 3.1 ISO 9001 Compliance Assessment

**Audit Questions:**
- [ ] Is quality policy documented?
- [ ] Are responsibilities defined?
- [ ] Are maintenance procedures documented?
- [ ] Is training documented?
- [ ] Is supplier evaluation performed?
- [ ] Are non-conformities tracked?
- [ ] Is CAPA process implemented?
- [ ] Are audits conducted?
- [ ] Is management review performed?
- [ ] Are documents controlled?

**Gap Analysis:**
- For each question, rate current state: 0 (absent), 1 (partial), 2 (present but not optimized), 3 (optimized)
- Identify gaps between current state and required state
- Assess risk of each gap

### 3.2 ISO 13485 Compliance Assessment

**Medical Device-Specific Requirements:**
- [ ] Is risk management performed (ISO 14971)?
- [ ] Is traceability maintained?
- [ ] Are suppliers evaluated and monitored?
- [ ] Are records retained appropriately?
- [ ] Is personnel competent?
- [ ] Are procedures validated?
- [ ] Is document control in place?
- [ ] Are post-maintenance activities managed?
- [ ] Is customer feedback collected?

### 3.3 ISO 14971 Risk Assessment

**Objective:** Assess current risk management maturity

**Risk Questions:**
- [ ] Are equipment hazards identified?
- [ ] Is risk assessed?
- [ ] Are controls implemented?
- [ ] Is residual risk evaluated?
- [ ] Is post-implementation monitoring in place?
- [ ] Are risk records maintained?

## 4. Initial Audit

### 4.1 Audit Scope

**What to Audit:**
- Organization structure and roles
- Existing procedures and their effectiveness
- Equipment maintenance documentation
- Staff competence and training
- Compliance with existing procedures
- Risk management practices
- Customer satisfaction
- Non-conformity management

### 4.2 Audit Activities

**Week 1: Planning**
1. Develop detailed audit plan
2. Identify areas to audit
3. Develop audit checklist
4. Brief audit team

**Week 2: On-Site Audit**
1. Conduct opening meeting
2. Review documentation
3. Interview staff
4. Observe activities
5. Conduct closing meeting
6. Compile findings

**Week 3: Report Preparation**
1. Organize findings
2. Determine severity levels
3. Document evidence
4. Draft report
5. Get feedback
6. Finalize report

### 4.3 Audit Findings

**Categorize Findings:**
- Strengths: Current practices that are effective
- Gaps: Missing requirements
- Non-conformities: Current practices that don't meet requirements
- Observations: Areas for improvement

**Document:**
- [ ] Initial Audit Report
- [ ] Findings Summary
- [ ] Evidence Documentation

## 11. Risk Management

### 11.1 Phase 1 Risks

**Risk:** Insufficient participation from operational staff
- **Impact:** Incomplete understanding of current state
- **Mitigation:** Clear communication of importance, management support

**Risk:** Reluctance to share information
- **Impact:** Incomplete data for analysis
- **Mitigation:** Assure confidentiality, frame as improvement opportunity

**Risk:** Unrealistic timeline expectations
- **Impact:** Project delays, staff frustration
- **Mitigation:** Clear communication of realistic timelines
