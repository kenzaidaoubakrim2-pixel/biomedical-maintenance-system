# Phase 2: System Structuring - MIAO-MB Implementation

## Overview
Phase 2 focuses on designing and documenting all systems, processes, and procedures needed for MIAO-MB. This is where the framework becomes concrete and operational.

**Duration:** 2-3 weeks  
**Lead:** Quality Manager / Maintenance Director  
**Key Deliverables:** Procedures, templates, forms, KPI definitions, training materials

---

## 1. Process Definition

### 1.1 Process Mapping

**Objective:** Document all maintenance processes

**Main Processes to Map:**
1. **Maintenance Planning Process**
   - From: Requirement identified
   - To: Plan approved and communicated

2. **Preventive Maintenance Process**
   - From: Scheduled date arrives
   - To: Equipment return to service

3. **Corrective Maintenance Process**
   - From: Failure reported
   - To: Equipment return to service and root cause analyzed

4. **Risk Management Process**
   - From: Equipment acquired or change made
   - To: Risk assessment completed and controls implemented

5. **CAPA Process**
   - From: Non-conformity identified
   - To: Effectiveness verified and closed

**For Each Process, Document:**
- [ ] Process name and objective
- [ ] Process steps (start to finish)
- [ ] Input requirements
- [ ] Output/deliverables
- [ ] Process owner
- [ ] Timeline
- [ ] Responsible parties at each step
- [ ] Decision points
- [ ] Quality criteria
- [ ] Process flow diagram (visual)

### 1.2 Process Approval

- [ ] Maintenance Director approval
- [ ] Quality Manager approval
- [ ] Staff feedback incorporated
- [ ] Executive review if major change

## 4. Equipment Criticality Classification

### 4.1 Criticality Matrix

**Objective:** Classify equipment based on risk to patient safety

**Severity Levels (1-4):**
1. No clinical impact
2. Minor inconvenience
3. Impact on diagnosis or treatment
4. Serious risk to patient

**Probability Levels (1-4):**
1. Rare (< 1 per year)
2. Occasional (1-4 per year)
3. Frequent (monthly)
4. Very frequent (weekly or more)

**Detectability Levels (1-4):**
1. Easy to detect
2. Can be detected
3. Difficult to detect
4. Not detectable before harm

**Risk Score = Severity × Probability × Detectability**

### 4.2 Risk Level Categories

| Score | Category | Maintenance Interval | Monitoring |
|-------|----------|----------------------|-----------|
| 1-9 | Low | Annual or less | Standard |
| 10-24 | Medium | Quarterly | Enhanced |
| 25-48 | High | Monthly | Intensive |
| 49-64 | Critical | Monthly or more | Very intensive |

### 4.3 Equipment Classification

**For Each Equipment:**
1. Assess severity of potential failure
2. Assess probability of failure
3. Assess detectability
4. Calculate risk score
5. Assign criticality level
6. Document justification
7. Determine maintenance intervals
8. Define monitoring requirements

**Output:** Criticality Matrix spreadsheet with all equipment classified

## 10. Risk Assessment for Phase 2

### 10.1 Phase 2 Risks

**Risk:** Procedure development takes too long
- **Mitigation:** Use templates, parallel development, clear deadlines

**Risk:** Staff resistance to new procedures
- **Mitigation:** Involve staff in development, clear communication

**Risk:** Procedures too complex
- **Mitigation:** Pilot testing, simplification, user feedback

**Risk:** System selection wrong
- **Mitigation:** Thorough evaluation, pilot testing
