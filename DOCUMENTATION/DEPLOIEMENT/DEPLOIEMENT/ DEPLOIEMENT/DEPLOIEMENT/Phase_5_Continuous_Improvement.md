# Phase 5: Continuous Improvement - Sustaining MIAO-MB Excellence

## Overview
Phase 5 is ongoing and permanent. This phase focuses on sustaining the MIAO-MB system, monitoring performance, and continuously improving results.

**Duration:** Ongoing (quarterly reviews, annual cycles)  
**Lead:** Quality Manager / Management  
**Key Activities:** KPI monitoring, audits, CAPA management, process improvement, management review

---

## 1. Ongoing Performance Monitoring

### 1.1 Daily Monitoring

**Maintenance Supervisor Reviews Daily:**
- [ ] Maintenance tasks scheduled for day
- [ ] Any failures reported overnight
- [ ] Any urgent issues
- [ ] GMAO status and alerts
- [ ] Equipment availability

**Actions Taken:**
- Dispatch technicians
- Prioritize work
- Address urgent issues
- Communicate with departments

### 1.2 Weekly Monitoring

**Quality Manager Reviews Weekly:**
- [ ] Intervention reports submitted
- [ ] Forms completed correctly
- [ ] Any patterns emerging
- [ ] KPI tracking progress
- [ ] Outstanding CAPA actions

**Actions Taken:**
- Follow up on incomplete records
- Address any deviations
- Provide coaching
- Update CAPA status

### 1.3 Monthly Monitoring

**Monthly KPI Review (Conducted by Quality Manager):**

**KPI Metrics Reviewed:**
- Preventive maintenance completion: ____%
- MTTR: ___ hours (target: minimize)
- MTBF: ___ hours (target: maximize)
- Equipment availability: ___% (target: > 98% critical)
- Recurrence rate: ___% (target: < 10%)
- Document compliance: ___% (target: > 95%)
- CAPA closure rate: ___% (target: > 90%)
- Customer satisfaction: ___% (target: > 85%)

**Analysis:**
1. Compare to target
2. Compare to previous month
3. Identify trends
4. Identify outliers
5. Investigate variances

**Actions if Below Target:**
- Identify root cause
- Develop improvement plan
- Implement corrective action
- Monitor progress
- Report to management

**Monthly Report:**
- [ ] KPI dashboard updated
- [ ] Trend chart prepared
- [ ] Analysis documented
- [ ] Actions identified
- [ ] Report distributed

### 1.4 Quarterly Management Review

**Quarterly Review Agenda:**

1. **KPI Performance** (Quarterly trends, not just monthly)
2. **Audit Results** (Recent internal audits)
3. **Non-Conformity Status** (Summary and trends)
4. **CAPA Status** (New, in-progress, closed)
5. **Customer Feedback** (Satisfaction, complaints)
6. **Risk Assessment Updates** (Any new or changing risks)
7. **Resource and Budget** (Adequacy assessment)
8. **Improvement Initiatives** (Progress on improvements)
9. **Training Needs** (Any gaps identified)
10. **Strategic Objectives** (Progress toward annual goals)

**Review Frequency:** Minimum quarterly (every 3 months)  
**Documentation:** Meeting minutes retained for 3 years

### 1.5 Annual Management Review

**Comprehensive Annual Review:**

**Prepared in Q4, Held in December/January**

**Contents:**
- Year-to-date KPI performance
- Annual trend analysis
- All audit findings summary
- All non-conformities summary
- All CAPA actions summary
- Annual customer satisfaction survey
- Year-to-date budget performance
- Training and competence assessment
- Equipment status and needs
- Improvement initiatives summary
- Risk assessment annual update
- Strategic objectives assessment
- Recommendations for next year

**Decisions Made:**
- Objectives for next year
- Resource allocation for next year
- Budget approval
- Strategic direction
- Improvement priorities

## 3. Continuous CAPA Management

### 3.1 CAPA Process Ongoing

**Non-Conformities Continuously:**
- Identified through audits
- Identified through operations
- Identified through customer feedback
- Identified through KPI monitoring

**CAPA Process:**
1. Identification → 2 days
2. Root cause analysis → 5 days
3. Corrective action development → 5 days
4. Implementation → varies (30 days target)
5. Effectiveness verification → 30 days after implementation
6. Closure → upon verified effectiveness

### 3.2 CAPA Effectiveness Monitoring

**Monthly Review:**
- Number of open CAPA
- Status of in-progress CAPA
- On-time closure rate (target: > 90%)
- Any CAPA taking too long?
- Any CAPA reopened for ineffectiveness?

**Quarterly Analysis:**
- Trend in non-conformity types
- Areas with recurring issues
- Effectiveness of corrective actions
- Recommendations for systemic changes

### 3.3 Lessons Learned

**Capture and Share:**
- What worked well?
- What could be improved?
- How can we prevent recurrence?
- What can other areas learn?
- Share at team meetings

## 4. Risk Management Review

### 4.1 Risk Monitoring

**Monthly (for High/Critical Risk Equipment):**
- Review equipment status
- Any new failures or issues?
- Are controls still effective?
- Any changes in risk level?
- Update risk register

**Quarterly:**
- Formal risk assessment update
- Any new risks identified?
- Any risks resolved?
- Any changes needed to controls?
- Any changes to maintenance intervals?

**Annually:**
- Comprehensive risk reassessment
- Incorporate field data from year
- Update risk scores
- Adjust maintenance intervals if needed
- Communicate changes to team

### 4.2 Risk Control Effectiveness

**Verify That Controls Are Working:**
1. Maintenance is being performed as planned?
2. Equipment failures are decreasing?
3. Residual risks are acceptable?
4. No new safety issues emerging?

**If Controls Not Effective:**
- Strengthen controls
- Increase maintenance frequency
- Add monitoring
- Consider equipment replacement
