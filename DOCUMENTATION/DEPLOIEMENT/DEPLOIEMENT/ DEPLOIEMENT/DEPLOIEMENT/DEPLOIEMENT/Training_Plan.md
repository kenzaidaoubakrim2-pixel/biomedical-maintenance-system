# MIAO-MB Training Plan

## Overview
This document outlines the comprehensive training program for MIAO-MB implementation, ensuring all staff have the knowledge and skills required to operate effectively within the new system.

---

## 1. Training Objectives

### General Objectives
- Understand MIAO-MB purpose and benefits
- Know own role and responsibilities
- Master required procedures and processes
- Use tools and systems correctly
- Contribute to continuous improvement
- Demonstrate competence in maintenance activities

### Role-Specific Objectives

**Maintenance Technicians:**
- Execute preventive and corrective maintenance
- Document activities accurately
- Analyze root causes
- Comply with safety and quality requirements
- Continuously improve skills

**Department Managers/Users:**
- Understand maintenance requirements
- Schedule equipment access effectively
- Cooperate with maintenance team
- Report issues appropriately

**Quality Manager:**
- Conduct internal audits
- Analyze performance data
- Manage CAPA process
- Review and improve systems

**Maintenance Director:**
- Plan and allocate resources
- Monitor performance
- Make strategic decisions
- Lead continuous improvement

---

## 2. Training Audiences and Requirements

### All Staff (4 hours initial + 4 hours refresher annually)

**MIAO-MB Overview Training**
- Duration: 2 hours
- Content:
  - Why MIAO-MB is important
  - ISO 9001, 13485, 14971 overview
  - Benefits to organization
  - What changes
  - Q&A

**Procedures and Documentation Training**
- Duration: 2 hours
- Content:
  - Key procedures overview
  - Your role in the system
  - Forms and documentation
  - Compliance requirements
  - Questions and support resources

### Maintenance Technicians (12 hours initial + 8 hours refresher annually)

**Detailed Procedure Training**
- Duration: 4 hours
- Content:
  - Preventive maintenance procedure in detail
  - Corrective maintenance procedure in detail
  - Risk assessment for your equipment
  - Documentation requirements
  - Hands-on practice with forms

**Equipment-Specific Training**
- Duration: 4 hours (varies by equipment)
- Content:
  - Equipment operation and function
  - Maintenance requirements
  - Common failures and solutions
  - Hands-on practice with equipment
  - Safety procedures

**System and Tools Training**
- Duration: 2 hours
- Content:
  - GMAO system operation
  - Data entry procedures
  - Report generation
  - KPI dashboard
  - Troubleshooting

**Root Cause Analysis and CAPA**
- Duration: 2 hours
- Content:
  - Non-conformity identification
  - Root cause analysis methods
  - Corrective action planning
  - Verification methods

### Department Managers (4 hours initial + 2 hours refresher annually)

**Maintenance Scheduling and Cooperation**
- Duration: 2 hours
- Content:
  - Preventive maintenance calendar
  - Scheduling requirements
  - Cooperation with maintenance
  - Issue reporting procedures

**Quality and Compliance**
- Duration: 2 hours
- Content:
  - Compliance expectations
  - Quality requirements
  - Equipment care
  - Safety during maintenance

### Quality Manager (16 hours initial + 8 hours refresher annually)

**Audit Techniques**
- Duration: 4 hours
- Content:
  - Audit methodology
  - Evidence gathering
  - Interview techniques
  - Finding classification
  - Report writing

**ISO Standard Requirements**
- Duration: 4 hours
- Content:
  - ISO 9001 detailed requirements
  - ISO 13485 medical device requirements
  - ISO 14971 risk management
  - Compliance verification

**CAPA Management**
- Duration: 4 hours
- Content:
  - Root cause analysis in depth
  - CAPA planning and tracking
  - Effectiveness verification
  - Trending and analysis

**KPI Analysis**
- Duration: 4 hours
- Content:
  - KPI calculations
  - Trend analysis
  - Data quality assessment
  - Performance reporting
  - Corrective action planning

### Maintenance Director (8 hours initial + 4 hours refresher annually)

**System Management**
- Duration: 4 hours
- Content:
  - MIAO-MB overview
  - Management role and responsibilities
  - Performance monitoring
  - Resource allocation
  - Decision making

**Strategic Planning and Improvement**
- Duration: 4 hours
- Content:
  - Long-term planning
  - Improvement identification
  - Risk management
  - Budget planning
  - Change management

## 3. Training Schedule

### Phase 2 (Before Rollout)

**Week 1:**
- [ ] Classroom MIAO-MB Overview (all staff) - 2 hours
- [ ] Procedures Training (technicians) - 4 hours
- [ ] System Training (technicians) - 2 hours

**Week 2:**
- [ ] Equipment-Specific Training (technicians) - 4 hours per technician
- [ ] Department Manager Training - 2 hours
- [ ] Quality/Audit Training (quality manager) - 4 hours

**Week 3:**
- [ ] Hands-on practice with forms and procedures - 2-4 hours
- [ ] System testing and practice - 2 hours
- [ ] Final Q&A and readiness assessment

### Phase 3 (During Rollout)

**Ongoing Support:**
- Daily: Help desk available for questions
- Weekly: Team meeting with Q&A
- As needed: One-on-one coaching

**Refresher Training:**
- Week 4: Brief refresher on common issues
- Week 6: System adjustments and updates training

### After Rollout

**Annual Training:**
- Refresher training (4 hours) for all staff
- Equipment-specific updates as needed
- Procedure updates as procedures change
- New staff training on schedule

## 4. Training Methods

### Classroom Training
- Instructor-led in classroom or virtual
- PowerPoint presentations
- Discussion and Q&A
- Handouts and reference materials

### Hands-On Training
- Practice with actual equipment
- Practice with forms and templates
- Supervised practice with real maintenance tasks
- Skill demonstration and assessment

### One-on-One Coaching
- For equipment-specific training
- For system troubleshooting
- For skill development
- For struggling staff

### Self-Paced Learning
- Online modules (if available)
- Procedure manuals and guides
- Reference materials
- Video demonstrations

### On-the-Job Training
- Pairing with experienced technician
- Supervised real maintenance tasks
- Gradual increase in independence
- Mentoring

## 5. Training Instructors

### Instructor Qualifications

**Required:**
- Deep knowledge of subject matter
- Experience with MIAO-MB or similar systems
- Ability to communicate clearly
- Patience and willingness to help
- Availability for training schedule

**Instructors:**

**MIAO-MB Overview:**
- Quality Manager or External Consultant

**Procedures:**
- Quality Manager + Maintenance Supervisor

**Equipment-Specific:**
- Senior Maintenance Technician (for each equipment type)

**System/GMAO:**
- IT Staff or GMAO Vendor Trainer

**Audit and CAPA:**
- Quality Manager + External Consultant (if needed)

### Instructor Preparation

- [ ] Review training content thoroughly
- [ ] Prepare presentation materials
- [ ] Practice delivery
- [ ] Prepare handouts
- [ ] Test any demonstrations
- [ ] Prepare Q&A responses

## 8. Training Effectiveness Evaluation

### Post-Training Survey

**Attendees Complete Immediately After:**
1. Was content clear and understandable? (1-5)
2. Was instructor knowledgeable? (1-5)
3. Pace appropriate? (1-5)
4. Materials helpful? (1-5)
5. Do you feel prepared? (1-5)
6. What could be improved? (open-ended)

### Knowledge Retention Assessment

**30 Days After Training:**
- Brief quiz on key concepts
- Observation of application
- Manager feedback on readiness
- Trainer feedback on performance

### Behavioral Change Assessment

**90 Days After Training:**
- Are staff applying what they learned?
- Are procedures being followed?
- Is documentation complete?
- Any issues or challenges?
- Adjustment or refresher training needed?

### Overall Program Evaluation

**Quarterly:**
- Training effectiveness survey
- KPI improvements tied to training
- Staff feedback
- Adjustments needed

## 9. Refresher and Ongoing Training

### Annual Refresher Training

**For All Staff:**
- 4-hour refresher covering:
  - Procedure updates
  - KPI results and trends
  - Lessons learned
  - Quality and safety reminders
  - New requirements

**Timing:** Q1 each year

### Specialized Refresher

**As Needed:**
- New equipment introduction training
- Procedure change training
- System update training
- New staff orientation training

**Duration:** 2-4 hours depending on topic

### Continuous Learning

**Encouraged:**
- Industry conferences
- Webinars and online courses
- Peer learning and job shadowing
- Certification programs
- Professional development

- [ ] Welcome and overview

**Week 2-3: Classroom Training**
- [ ] MIAO-MB Overview (same as all staff)

**Week 4-8: Equipment Training**
