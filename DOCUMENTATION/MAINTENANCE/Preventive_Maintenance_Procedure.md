# Preventive Maintenance Procedure - MIAO-MB Model

## 1. Purpose and Scope

### Purpose
This procedure defines the requirements for planning, scheduling, executing, and documenting preventive maintenance activities on biomedical equipment to ensure optimal performance, patient safety, and regulatory compliance.

### Scope
This procedure applies to all biomedical equipment in the organization's inventory, including:
- Critical equipment (emergency response)
- Essential equipment (daily operations)
- Important equipment (support functions)

### Standards Referenced
- ISO 9001:2015 - Quality Management
- ISO 13485:2016 - Medical Device Quality Management
- ISO 14971:2019 - Risk Management

---

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **Preventive Maintenance** | Planned maintenance performed at predetermined intervals to maintain equipment in proper working condition and prevent failures |
| **Maintenance Interval** | Time period (in days, hours, or cycles) between preventive maintenance tasks |
| **MTBF** | Mean Time Between Failures - average operating time between equipment failures |
| **Availability** | Percentage of time equipment is operational and available for use |
| **Critical Equipment** | Equipment where failure poses direct risk to patient safety |
| **Essential Equipment** | Equipment required for normal daily operations |
| **Technician** | Qualified personnel authorized to perform maintenance |

---

## 3. Responsibilities

### Maintenance Director
- Approve annual preventive maintenance plan
- Allocate resources and budget
- Review performance metrics
- Authorize deviations from schedule
- Conduct management review

### Maintenance Supervisor
- Develop detailed maintenance schedules
- Assign maintenance tasks to technicians
- Monitor schedule compliance
- Investigate deviations
- Track completion rates

### Maintenance Technician
- Perform preventive maintenance as scheduled
- Document all activities on intervention form
- Report equipment abnormalities
- Use only approved spare parts
- Maintain safety and cleanliness

### Equipment User/Department
- Schedule maintenance access with Maintenance Supervisor
- Provide equipment availability information
- Report equipment issues promptly
- Support maintenance activities
- Return equipment to service

### Quality Manager
- Audit preventive maintenance execution
- Review documentation completeness
- Track non-conformities
- Verify effectiveness through KPI analysis
- Recommend improvements

## 4. Annual Preventive Maintenance Planning

### Step 1: Equipment Inventory Review (January)
1. List all biomedical equipment
2. Verify serial numbers and locations
3. Update equipment specifications
4. Confirm equipment is still in use
5. Document any retirements or additions

### Step 2: Maintenance Interval Determination (January)
For each equipment:
1. Review manufacturer recommendations
2. Assess criticality level
3. Consider historical failure data
4. Factor in regulatory requirements
5. Document maintenance intervals

**Standard Intervals:**
- Critical equipment: Monthly to quarterly
- Essential equipment: Quarterly to semi-annually
- Important equipment: Semi-annually to annually

### Step 3: Resource Planning (February)
1. Calculate total maintenance hours needed
2. Assess technician capacity
3. Identify training needs
4. Plan spare parts procurement
5. Allocate budget

### Step 4: Schedule Development (February)
1. Create annual maintenance calendar
2. Distribute tasks throughout the year
3. Avoid scheduling conflicts
4. Plan maintenance windows during low-use periods
5. Document in Maintenance Schedule spreadsheet

### Step 5: Approval and Communication (March)
1. Present plan to management
2. Get executive approval
3. Communicate to all departments
4. Provide calendar to equipment users
5. Post schedules at equipment locations

---

## 5. Monthly Maintenance Scheduling

### First Week of Month
1. Confirm equipment availability
2. Schedule specific maintenance dates
3. Notify affected departments
4. Reserve necessary resources
5. Prepare maintenance kits

### Schedule Contents
- Equipment name and ID
- Planned maintenance date
- Technician assigned
- Estimated duration
- Department to notify

### Deviation Management
If scheduled maintenance cannot occur:
1. Document reason for deviation
2. Determine if rescheduling is required
3. Assess impact on equipment safety
4. Communicate with Maintenance Supervisor
5. Update schedule within 5 business days

## 6. Preventive Maintenance Execution

### Pre-Maintenance Preparation (Day Before)

**Technician Responsibilities:**
1. Review equipment manual and procedures
2. Gather required tools and parts
3. Inspect equipment documentation
4. Prepare intervention report form
5. Verify safety procedures

**Department Notification:**
1. Confirm equipment availability
2. Arrange access if needed
3. Plan for alternative equipment if critical
4. Brief staff on maintenance window

### Maintenance Execution

**Personal Protective Equipment:**
- Wear appropriate PPE per procedure
- Ensure safety of surrounding environment
- Follow infection control measures

**Maintenance Steps:**
1. Verify equipment identity and serial number
2. Document current status (working/not working)
3. Perform all required maintenance tasks per checklist
4. Note any abnormalities observed
5. Replace parts as specified
6. Document all work performed

**Testing and Verification:**
1. Perform functionality tests per procedure
2. Verify calibration if required
3. Document test results
4. Confirm all safety systems functional
5. Return equipment to service

**Documentation:**
1. Complete Intervention Report form
2. Record date, time, and technician name
3. Document tasks performed
4. Record any issues identified
5. Sign and date form

### Post-Maintenance

1. **Equipment Inspection:**
   - Visual inspection for cleanliness
   - Verify all covers and seals are in place
   - Check for any visible damage

2. **Return to Service:**
   - Notify equipment user that maintenance is complete
   - Provide any usage instructions
   - Document return-to-service time

3. **Follow-up:**
   - Monitor for any issues in first week
   - Be available for questions

## 7. Maintenance Task Checklist

### Monthly Maintenance Tasks (Example for Sterilizer)

**Internal Inspection:**
- [ ] Check water inlet filter
- [ ] Inspect chamber door seals
- [ ] Verify drain line is clear
- [ ] Check steam supply connections

**Functional Test:**
- [ ] Run test cycle
- [ ] Verify temperature readings
- [ ] Verify pressure readings
- [ ] Verify time display

**Cleaning:**
- [ ] Clean external surfaces
- [ ] Clean chamber interior
- [ ] Clean door seals
- [ ] Clean drain trap

**Documentation:**
- [ ] Record all findings
- [ ] Note any repairs needed
- [ ] Update equipment log

### Quarterly Maintenance Tasks (Example for Sterilizer)

**All monthly tasks plus:**
- [ ] Calibration verification
- [ ] Safety system test
- [ ] Air removal system verification
- [ ] Load capacity test

### Annual Maintenance Tasks (Example for Sterilizer)

**All quarterly tasks plus:**
- [ ] Comprehensive system inspection
- [ ] Part replacement per schedule
- [ ] Major cleaning and refurbishment
- [ ] Full functional test
- [ ] Regulatory compliance check

## 8. Documentation Requirements

### Intervention Report Form Must Include:
- Equipment identification (name, ID, serial number)
- Maintenance date and time
- Technician name and signature
- Type of maintenance (preventive, corrective)
- Tasks performed
- Equipment status before and after
- Parts replaced (part numbers and serial numbers)
- Test results
- Any abnormalities noted
- Next maintenance due date
- Technician comments

### Documentation Storage:
- Original filed in equipment maintenance folder
- Copy provided to department
- Electronic copy entered in maintenance system
- Retained per document retention policy (minimum 2 years)

## 9. Performance Monitoring

### Key Performance Indicators

| KPI | Formula | Target | Frequency |
|-----|---------|--------|-----------|
| Schedule Compliance | Completed / Planned × 100 | > 95% | Monthly |
| MTBF | Operating time / Failures | Increasing | Monthly |
| Availability | Available time / Required time × 100 | > 98% critical | Monthly |
| Preventive Effectiveness | Failures prevented / Total interventions | > 80% | Quarterly |

### Monthly KPI Review:
1. Calculate compliance percentage
2. Identify missed or overdue tasks
3. Analyze reasons for deviations
4. Implement corrective measures
5. Report to management

### Quarterly Performance Analysis:
1. Trend analysis of compliance
2. Equipment-specific analysis
3. Technician performance review
4. Resource adequacy assessment
5. Budget expenditure review

---

## 10. Non-Compliance Management

### Missed Maintenance Tasks

**Immediate Actions:**
1. Identify reason for delay
2. Determine risk to patient safety
3. Prioritize rescheduling
4. Adjust schedule if necessary
5. Document in compliance log

**Investigation:**
1. Was equipment unavailable?
2. Were resources insufficient?
3. Was technician unavailable?
4. What can be improved?

**Corrective Action:**
1. Address root cause
2. Implement preventive measure
3. Monitor to prevent recurrence

### Equipment Failure Between Scheduled Maintenance

1. Document as incident
2. Perform root cause analysis
3. Determine if maintenance plan was adequate
4. Adjust future maintenance intervals if needed
5. Update risk assessment
