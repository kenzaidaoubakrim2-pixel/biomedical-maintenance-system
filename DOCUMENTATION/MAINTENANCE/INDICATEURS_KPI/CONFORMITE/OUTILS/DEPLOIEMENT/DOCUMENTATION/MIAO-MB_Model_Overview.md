# MIAO-MB: Integrated Model for Audit and Optimization of Biomedical Maintenance

## 1. Title
**MIAO-MB: Integrated Model for Audit, Analysis, Optimization and Continuous Improvement of Biomedical Maintenance for a PME Services Company**

Aligned with ISO 9001, ISO 13485, and ISO 14971

## 2. General Objective
Design an integrated model that enables a biomedical services PME to evaluate, control, optimize, and continuously improve its biomedical maintenance activities while ensuring compliance with quality, regulatory, and risk management requirements.

## 3. Specific Objectives
- Evaluate the maturity of the biomedical maintenance system
- Identify gaps against ISO 9001, ISO 13485, and ISO 14971 requirements
- Classify biomedical equipment according to criticality
- Implement risk analysis applied to maintenance activities
- Optimize preventive and corrective maintenance
- Define performance indicators suited to a PME
- Structure a continuous improvement process based on audits, KPIs, non-conformities, and CAPA

## 4. Reference Standards

### ISO 9001:2015 - Quality Management System
Provides the general framework for quality management system with emphasis on:
- Process approach
- Customer satisfaction
- Leadership
- Risk management
- Continuous improvement

### ISO 13485:2016 - Medical Device Quality Management
Specific to medical devices, emphasizing:
- Document control and management
- Traceability
- Regulatory requirements
- Personnel competence
- Process validation
- Supplier monitoring
- Medical device safety

### ISO 14971:2019 - Risk Management
Defines risk management methodology for medical devices covering:
- Hazard identification
- Risk estimation and evaluation
- Risk control
- Residual risk assessment
- Post-implementation monitoring

## 5. Model Principles
The MIAO-MB model is based on four pillars:

### Audit
Verify the existence, application, and effectiveness of maintenance practices

### Analysis
Identify causes of gaps, risks, and system weaknesses

### Optimization
Improve organization, resources, methods, and performance

### Continuous Improvement
Sustainably drive performance through KPIs, CAPA, reviews, and feedback

## 6. General Model Architecture
Biomedical Park Mapping ↓ Initial Audit ↓ Gap Analysis ↓ Risk Analysis ↓ Performance Diagnosis ↓ Optimization Plan ↓ Implementation ↓ KPI Monitoring ↓ CAPA & Continuous Improvement

## 7. Integrated Model Processes

| Process | Purpose | Associated Standards |
|---------|---------|----------------------|
| Biomedical Park Management | Identify, classify and track all equipment | ISO 9001, ISO 13485 |
| Preventive Maintenance | Reduce failures and prevent risks | ISO 13485, ISO 14971 |
| Corrective Maintenance | Restore availability and document intervention | ISO 9001, ISO 13485 |
| Risk Management | Identify, evaluate and control risks | ISO 14971 |
| Document Management | Ensure traceability and proof of compliance | ISO 9001, ISO 13485 |
| Competence Management | Guarantee technician aptitude | ISO 9001, ISO 13485 |
| Parts and Supplier Management | Control procurement and compliance | ISO 13485 |
| Internal Audit | Evaluate compliance and system effectiveness | ISO 9001, ISO 13485 |
| CAPA | Address causes of gaps and prevent recurrence | ISO 9001, ISO 13485 |
| Management Review | Decide priorities and resources | ISO 9001, ISO 13485 |

## 8. Key Deliverables

- Biomedical process mapping
- Equipment inventory
- Criticality matrix
- Annual preventive maintenance plan
- Preventive maintenance procedure
- Corrective maintenance procedure
- Standardized intervention form
- Non-conformity register
- CAPA register
- KPI dashboard
- Internal audit plan
- Audit report
- Management review minutes

## 9. Implementation Success Factors

1. **Leadership commitment** - Management support and resources
2. **Staff competence** - Training and qualification
3. **Document control** - Clear and accessible procedures
4. **Risk consciousness** - Understanding of criticality
5. **Data management** - Accurate records and traceability
6. **Continuous monitoring** - Regular KPI review
7. **Proactive improvement** - Acting on findings
8. **Communication** - Clear and transparent information flow
