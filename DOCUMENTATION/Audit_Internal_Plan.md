# Audit Internal Plan - MIAO-MB Model

## Overview
The internal audit plan defines the schedule, scope, and methodology for conducting internal audits to ensure compliance with ISO 9001, ISO 13485, and ISO 14971 requirements.

## Annual Audit Schedule

### Q1 (January - March)
**Audit 1: Organization and Governance**
- Date: Week 2 of March
- Duration: 2 days
- Scope: Roles, responsibilities, planning, competencies
- Auditor: External or experienced internal auditor
- Focus Areas: ISO 9001 §5, ISO 13485 §4-5

**Audit 2: Document Management**
- Date: Week 4 of March
- Duration: 1 day
- Scope: Procedures, records, document control
- Auditor: Quality manager
- Focus Areas: ISO 9001 §8.2, ISO 13485 §4.2

### Q2 (April - June)
**Audit 3: Maintenance Performance**
- Date: Week 2 of May
- Duration: 2 days
- Scope: Preventive and corrective maintenance, KPIs
- Auditor: Maintenance supervisor
- Focus Areas: ISO 13485 §8.5, Preventive maintenance effectiveness

**Audit 4: Risk Management**
- Date: Week 4 of May
- Duration: 1.5 days
- Scope: Risk analysis, controls, residual risk
- Auditor: Quality manager or risk coordinator
- Focus Areas: ISO 14971, Risk assessment documentation

### Q3 (July - September)
**Audit 5: Supplier Management**
- Date: Week 2 of August
- Duration: 1 day
- Scope: Supplier evaluation, parts control, agreements
- Auditor: Procurement or quality manager
- Focus Areas: ISO 13485 §8.4, Supplier compliance

**Audit 6: Competence and Training**
- Date: Week 4 of August
- Duration: 1 day
- Scope: Training records, competence assessment, qualifications
- Auditor: HR or training coordinator
- Focus Areas: ISO 9001 §7.2, ISO 13485 §6.2

### Q4 (October - December)
**Audit 7: CAPA and Non-Conformity**
- Date: Week 2 of November
- Duration: 1.5 days
- Scope: Non-conformity register, CAPA tracking, effectiveness
- Auditor: Quality manager
- Focus Areas: ISO 9001 §10.2, ISO 13485 §8.3

**Audit 8: Post-Maintenance Monitoring**
- Date: Week 4 of November
- Duration: 1 day
- Scope: Equipment performance, customer feedback, field data
- Auditor: Maintenance supervisor
- Focus Areas: ISO 13485 §7.5.5, Post-delivery activities

## Audit Methodology

### Pre-Audit Phase (1 week before)
1. **Audit notification** - Inform department
2. **Document review** - Collect and review documents
3. **Audit plan** - Prepare detailed audit plan
4. **Checklist preparation** - Finalize audit checklist
5. **Auditor briefing** - Prepare audit team

### Audit Phase (On-site)

**Opening Meeting**
- Objectives and scope
- Schedule and procedures
- Questions and concerns

**Audit Activities**
- Document review
- Process observation
- Staff interviews
- Evidence collection

**Closing Meeting**
- Findings summary
- Observations and minor issues
- Major findings discussion
- Next steps

### Post-Audit Phase (1-2 weeks after)

1. **Report preparation** - Document all findings
2. **Corrective action request** - For major findings
3. **Distribution** - Share report with management
4. **Follow-up plan** - Schedule corrective actions
5. **Verification** - Confirm actions taken

## Audit Findings Classification

### Minor Finding (Non-Conformity)
- Isolated non-compliance
- Procedure not followed in one instance
- No impact on quality or safety
- Action: Corrective action recommended
- Timeline: Within 30 days

### Major Finding (Non-Conformity)
- Systemic non-compliance
- Procedure not followed consistently
- Potential impact on quality or safety
- Action: Corrective action required
- Timeline: Within 15 days

### Critical Finding (Non-Conformity)
- Serious non-compliance with ISO requirements
- Direct impact on patient safety or regulatory compliance
- Immediate action required
- Action: Immediate corrective action
- Timeline: Within 3-5 days

### Observation (Not a finding)
- Area for improvement
- Current practice not optimal
- No non-compliance
- Action: Continuous improvement opportunity
- Timeline: As part of planned improvements

## Audit Resources Required

- **Auditors:** 1-2 per audit (depending on scope)
- **Time:** 8-12 auditor-days per year
- **Budget:** Training, travel, documentation
- **Tools:** Audit checklist, recording equipment
- **Documentation:** Audit reports, findings log

## Audit Competence Requirements

### Auditor Qualifications
- Understanding of ISO 9001, ISO 13485, ISO 14971
- Knowledge of biomedical maintenance operations
- Interview and communication skills
- Objectivity and impartiality
- Experience in audit processes

### Auditor Training
- Initial audit training (16 hours)
- Specific standard training (8 hours)
- Annual refresher training (4 hours)
- On-the-job mentoring (with experienced auditor)

## Performance Metrics for Audit Program

| Metric | Target | Measurement |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| Audit completion on schedule | 100% | Calendar tracking |
| Corrective actions on time | > 95% | CAPA register |
| Audit finding closure | > 90% in 30 days | Follow-up audit |
| Preventive effectiveness | > 80% | Non-conformity trend |
| Audit report quality | > 4/5 | Management assessment |

## Risk-Based Audit Approach

High-risk areas receive more frequent audits:
- Critical equipment maintenance
- Patient safety procedures
- Regulatory compliance areas
- High non-conformity areas

## Continuous Improvement of Audit Process

**Annual Review:**
- Effectiveness of audit program
- Findings trends analysis
- Auditor feedback
- Process improvements

**Update triggers:**
- New regulatory requirements
- Major findings
- Process changes
- Management direction
