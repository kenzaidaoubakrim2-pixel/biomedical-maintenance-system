# ISO 9001:2015 Requirements for Biomedical Maintenance

## Overview
ISO 9001:2015 establishes requirements for a quality management system (QMS) applicable to organizations of any size and sector. For biomedical maintenance, it provides the framework for systematic quality management.

## Key Requirements

### 1. Context of the Organization
The organization must determine external and internal issues relevant to biomedical maintenance:
- Regulatory requirements for medical devices
- Customer requirements (hospitals, clinics)
- Supplier performance
- Equipment performance history
- Competitive landscape

### 2. Leadership and Commitment
- **Quality policy** must be documented and communicated
- **Roles and responsibilities** must be clearly defined
- **Management review** must be conducted periodically

### 3. Planning
**3.1 Risks and Opportunities:**
- Identify risks to maintenance effectiveness
- Identify opportunities for improvement
- Plan actions to address risks

**3.2 Quality Objectives and Planning:**
- Define measurable quality objectives
- Plan to achieve objectives
- Communicate objectives to personnel

### 4. Support

**4.1 Resources:**
- Allocate adequate personnel, infrastructure, and environment
- Provide maintenance equipment and tools
- Ensure budget availability

**4.2 Competence:**
- Determine required competences
- Provide training and development
- Maintain competence records

**4.3 Awareness:**
- Ensure personnel understand quality policy
- Understand contribution to objectives
- Understand non-conformity implications

**4.4 Communication:**
- Establish communication processes
- Decide what, when, how, and to whom
- Maintain internal and external communication records

**4.5 Documented Information:**
- Create and maintain documentation required
- Control document versions
- Prevent unauthorized use of obsolete documents
- Protect confidential information

### 5. Operation

**5.1 Operational Planning and Control:**
- Plan maintenance activities
- Define process requirements and criteria
- Provide documented procedures
- Control process changes
- Communicate requirements to suppliers

**5.2 Requirements for Products and Services:**
- Understand customer requirements
- Document service requirements
- Review before delivery
- Manage customer property and information

**5.3 Maintenance Service Delivery:**
- Ensure all planned arrangements are implemented
- Control service modifications
- Document who performed the service
- Document customer feedback

**5.4 Control of Externally Provided Processes:**
- Evaluate and monitor subcontractors
- Define requirements for external maintenance providers
- Maintain records of evaluations

### 6. Performance Evaluation

**6.1 Monitoring and Measurement:**
- Monitor maintenance performance
- Track equipment availability rates
- Measure MTTR and MTBF
- Conduct internal audits
- Measure customer satisfaction
- Record and analyze data

**6.2 Internal Audit:**
- Conduct internal audits at planned intervals
- Audit against ISO 9001 requirements
- Verify process effectiveness
- Document findings and follow-up

**6.3 Management Review:**
- Conduct periodic reviews (at least annually)
- Review audit results and KPIs
- Review customer feedback
- Discuss improvement opportunities
- Document decisions and actions

### 7. Improvement

**7.1 Non-Conformity and Corrective Action:**
- Identify non-conformities through audits and monitoring
- Determine root causes
- Implement corrective actions
- Verify effectiveness
- Update risk assessments

**7.2 Continual Improvement:**
- Improve effectiveness of QMS
- Improve maintenance processes
- Improve equipment performance
- Document improvement initiatives

## Implementation Checklist for Biomedical Maintenance

- [ ] Quality policy documented and communicated
- [ ] Roles and responsibilities defined
- [ ] Maintenance procedures documented
- [ ] Training records maintained
- [ ] Equipment and resources allocated
- [ ] Risk and opportunity assessment completed
- [ ] Quality objectives set and tracked
- [ ] Supplier evaluation process in place
- [ ] Non-conformity register established
- [ ] CAPA process implemented
- [ ] KPI monitoring system operational
- [ ] Internal audit schedule defined
- [ ] Management review conducted quarterly
- [ ] Customer satisfaction measured
- [ ] Document control system in place
- [ ] Continuous improvement log maintained
