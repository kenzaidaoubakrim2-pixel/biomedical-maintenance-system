# ISO 13485:2016 Requirements for Biomedical Maintenance

## Overview
ISO 13485:2016 specifies requirements for a quality management system for organizations that design, manufacture, and maintain medical devices. For biomedical maintenance services, compliance is critical for patient safety and regulatory compliance.

## Key Requirements Specific to Biomedical Maintenance

### 1. Risk Management
**ISO 14971 Integration:**
- Risk analysis must be performed for all maintenance activities
- Identify hazards that could result from inadequate maintenance
- Evaluate risks associated with equipment failures
- Implement risk control measures
- Monitor residual risks

### 2. Design and Development (if applicable)
- Document maintenance procedures
- Validate procedures against requirements
- Review and approve procedures
- Control procedure changes

### 3. Document Control
**Critical for Biomedical Maintenance:**
- Maintain master list of applicable documents
- Control document approval and authorization
- Prevent use of obsolete documents
- Archive superseded documents
- Version control all procedures

**Required Documents:**
- Maintenance procedures
- Intervention reports
- Equipment specifications
- Training records
- Audit reports

### 4. Traceability Requirements

**Equipment Traceability:**
- Unique identification of each equipment
- Serial number tracking
- Installation date and location
- Maintenance history documentation
- Supplier information

**Service Traceability:**
- Identification of technician performing service
- Date and time of intervention
- Description of work performed
- Parts used and serial numbers
- Results of testing

### 5. Supplier Management
- Evaluate and select suppliers of spare parts
- Define requirements for suppliers
- Monitor supplier performance
- Maintain supplier records
- Establish supplier agreements

### 6. Production and Service Provision Control

**Maintenance Service Control:**
- Plan maintenance activities
- Define work instructions
- Establish quality criteria
- Use calibrated instruments
- Document all activities
- Verify service completion

**Quality Control:**
- Implement preventive measures
- Perform equipment testing after maintenance
- Document test results
- Verify correct installation
- Conduct functional tests

### 7. Identification and Traceability
- Identify equipment and services throughout maintenance
- Maintain unique identifiers
- Preserve traceability from receipt to completion
- Document all changes

### 8. Storage, Preservation, and Delivery
- Store equipment safely
- Prevent damage during handling
- Use appropriate storage conditions
- Document storage status
- Maintain equipment readiness

### 9. Control of Customer Property
- Identify customer property (equipment provided for maintenance)
- Protect customer property from loss or damage
- Record receipt and condition
- Document all maintenance
- Return in acceptable condition

### 10. Post-Delivery Activities

**Post-Maintenance Support:**
- Monitor equipment performance after maintenance
- Collect performance data
- Analyze field data
- Document any issues
- Provide feedback to improvement process

**Customer Feedback:**
- Collect information from customers
- Analyze feedback for trends
- Identify improvement opportunities
- Communicate results

### 11. Personnel Competence
**Critical Requirement:**
- Define required competencies
- Provide training on maintenance procedures
- Ensure understanding of ISO 13485 requirements
- Document training completion
- Maintain competence records
- Provide periodic refresher training

### 12. Record Control and Retention
**Required Records:**
- Maintenance records (minimum 2 years or longer)
- Training records
- Audit findings
- CAPA records
- Supplier evaluations
- Equipment performance data
- Customer feedback

**Record Requirements:**
- Legible and readily retrievable
- Protected from deterioration
- Secured against unauthorized access
- Retention period defined
- Disposal method documented

### 13. Reporting Adverse Events
- Monitor equipment performance
- Identify malfunctions or failures
- Evaluate potential patient impact
- Report to regulatory authorities if required
- Document all adverse events

## ISO 13485 vs ISO 9001: Key Differences for Maintenance

| Aspect | ISO 9001 | ISO 13485 |
|--------|----------|----------|
| **Scope** | Any organization | Medical device organizations |
| **Risk Management** | General risk approach | ISO 14971 required |
| **Traceability** | Required | Critical for medical devices |
| **Supplier Control** | Required | Enhanced requirements |
| **Post-market Surveillance** | Not required | Required |
| **Adverse Event Reporting** | Not required | Required if applicable |
| **Design Control** | Not required | Required if applicable |
| **Sterilization/Contamination** | Not specific | Critical requirement |
