# ISO 14971:2019 Risk Management for Medical Devices

## Overview
ISO 14971:2019 establishes requirements for the application of risk management to medical devices and maintenance activities. It's a critical standard for biomedical maintenance operations.

## Risk Management Process

### 1. Risk Analysis

**Step 1: Identify Hazards**
- What could go wrong with the equipment?
- What could cause incorrect maintenance?
- What could lead to patient harm?
- What environmental factors are involved?

**Step 2: Identify Hazardous Situations**
- What conditions could result from the hazard?
- How could the hazard manifest?
- Under what circumstances would it occur?

**Step 3: Identify Potential Harm**
- What injury or damage could result?
- What is the severity?
- What is the probability?

**Step 4: Estimate Risk**
Risk = Severity × Probability × Detectability

### 2. Risk Evaluation

**Risk Assessment Criteria:**
- **Severity (1-4):**
  - 1 = No clinical impact
  - 2 = Minor inconvenience
  - 3 = Impact on diagnosis or treatment
  - 4 = Serious risk to patient

- **Probability (1-4):**
  - 1 = Rare
  - 2 = Occasional
  - 3 = Frequent
  - 4 = Very frequent

- **Detectability (1-4):**
  - 1 = Easy to detect
  - 2 = Can be detected
  - 3 = Difficult to detect
  - 4 = Not detectable before effect

**Risk Levels:**
- **1-9:** Low risk (acceptable)
- **10-24:** Medium risk (enhanced monitoring)
- **25-48:** High risk (priority action)
- **49-64:** Critical risk (immediate action)

### 3. Risk Control

**Control Options:**
1. **Eliminate the hazard** (design change)
2. **Reduce probability** (preventive maintenance)
3. **Reduce severity** (redundancy, safeguards)
4. **Inform and educate** (training, labeling)

**Verification of Controls:**
- Test that control is effective
- Verify through testing or inspection
- Document control verification
- Maintain control effectiveness

### 4. Residual Risk Evaluation

**After Controls Implemented:**
- Re-estimate risk with controls in place
- Verify that residual risk is acceptable
- Compare to risk acceptance criteria
- Document decision

### 5. Risk Management Review

**Conducted When:**
- Design changes are made
- New equipment is acquired
- Maintenance procedures are modified
- Field data indicates new hazards
- After significant incidents

## Risk Management for Maintenance Activities

### Maintenance-Specific Hazards

**Preventive Maintenance:**
- Incorrect calibration
- Missed maintenance intervals
- Improper parts installation
- Use of non-approved components
- Inadequate technician training

**Corrective Maintenance:**
- Equipment failure to diagnose
- Wrong part replacement
- Incomplete repair
- Safety system disablement during repair
- Extended downtime

**Documentation:**
- Lost maintenance records
- Incomplete documentation
- Incorrect information
- Unauthorized procedure changes

## Risk Management Documentation

### Required Documents

**Risk Management File:**
- Risk analysis forms
- Risk evaluation records
- Risk control measures
- Verification of controls
- Residual risk assessments
- Management review findings

**Risk Register:**
- List of identified risks
- Risk level (before and after controls)
- Responsible parties
- Target completion dates
- Verification records

**Equipment Risk Assessment:**
For each critical equipment:
- Equipment identification
- Known hazards
- Historical failure data
- Risk score
- Applied controls
- Monitoring requirements

## Risk Management Plan

**Annual Review:**
- Analyze adverse events
- Review field complaints
- Evaluate near-miss incidents
- Identify emerging risks
- Update risk assessments
- Document decisions

**Post-Implementation Monitoring:**
- Monitor equipment performance
- Track failure rates
- Analyze maintenance effectiveness
- Identify control failures
- Report findings to management

## Integration with Quality Management

**Link to ISO 9001:**
- Risk management inputs to quality objectives
- Risk assessments inform audit plans
- Risk evaluations drive improvement priorities
- Risk register reviewed in management review

**Link to ISO 13485:**
- Risk management guides training needs
- Risk assessments determine supplier requirements
- Risk-based document control
- Risk-based traceability requirements
