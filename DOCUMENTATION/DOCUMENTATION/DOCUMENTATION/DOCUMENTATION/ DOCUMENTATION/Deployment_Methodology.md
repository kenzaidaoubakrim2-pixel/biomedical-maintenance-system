# MIAO-MB Deployment Methodology

## Overview
This document outlines the step-by-step approach to implementing the MIAO-MB model in a biomedical maintenance PME.

## Implementation Timeline
- **Phase 1:** 2-3 weeks
- **Phase 2:** 2-3 weeks
- **Phase 3:** 4-6 weeks
- **Phase 4:** 2 weeks
- **Phase 5:** Ongoing

**Total Initial Implementation: 10-15 weeks**

## Phase 1: Initial Diagnosis (Weeks 1-3)

### Objective
Understand the current state of biomedical maintenance and identify gaps against MIAO-MB requirements.

### Activities

**1.1 Stakeholder Engagement**
- Identify project sponsor and team
- Define roles and responsibilities
- Schedule kickoff meeting
- Communicate objectives to all staff

**1.2 Document Collection**
- Gather existing maintenance procedures
- Collect equipment documentation
- Review maintenance records
- Identify all process documentation

**1.3 Equipment Inventory**
- List all biomedical equipment
- Record serial numbers and locations
- Identify equipment age and condition
- Document maintenance history

**1.4 Initial Audit**
- Assess compliance with ISO 9001
- Assess compliance with ISO 13485
- Assess compliance with ISO 14971
- Document findings and gaps

**1.5 Stakeholder Interviews**
- Interview maintenance technicians
- Interview equipment users
- Interview management
- Document pain points and concerns

### Deliverables
- Equipment inventory list
- Initial audit report
- Gap analysis document
- Stakeholder feedback summary
- Project charter

---

## Phase 2: System Structuring (Weeks 4-6)

### Objective
Design and establish the foundational systems and structures of MIAO-MB.

### Activities

**2.1 Process Definition**
- Define maintenance processes
- Create process flow diagrams
- Document process steps
- Identify process owners

**2.2 Procedure Development**
- Write preventive maintenance procedure
- Write corrective maintenance procedure
- Write risk management procedure
- Write audit procedure
- Write CAPA procedure

**2.3 Template Creation**
- Create intervention report template
- Create risk assessment form
- Create audit checklist
- Create CAPA form
- Create non-conformity register

**2.4 Equipment Criticality**
- Classify equipment by criticality
- Identify critical equipment for patient safety
- Assign risk levels
- Document justification

**2.5 KPI Definition**
- Define relevant KPIs
- Establish baseline measurements
- Set improvement targets
- Create measurement method

**2.6 Competence Assessment**
- Assess current technician competencies
- Identify training needs
- Plan training program
- Document competence requirements

### Deliverables
- Process documentation (5 main processes)
- Procedures (5 procedures)
- Templates and forms (8 templates)
- Criticality matrix
- KPI definitions
- Training plan

---

## Phase 3: Implementation (Weeks 7-12)

### Objective
Execute the designed systems and embed them into daily operations.

### Activities

**3.1 Staff Training**
- Conduct initial training on MIAO-MB
- Train on new procedures
- Train on templates and forms
- Conduct hands-on practice

**3.2 System Initialization**
- Activate preventive maintenance schedule
- Initialize maintenance database/system
- Start using intervention forms
- Begin collecting maintenance data

**3.3 Pilot Program**
- Select critical equipment for pilot
- Apply full MIAO-MB process
- Test procedures and forms
- Gather feedback
- Refine based on feedback

**3.4 Full Rollout**
- Extend to all equipment
- Train all staff on new procedures
- Activate all preventive maintenance
- Begin comprehensive KPI tracking

**3.5 Documentation Management**
- Establish document control system
- Version control procedures
- Archive old documents
- Communicate document updates

**3.6 Risk Management Implementation**
- Perform risk analysis for critical equipment
- Complete risk assessment forms
- Document risk controls
- Establish monitoring procedures

### Deliverables
- Training completion records
- Risk assessment database
- Maintenance schedule active
- Completed intervention forms
- KPI baseline data collected

---

## Phase 4: Evaluation (Weeks 13-14)

### Objective
Assess the effectiveness of the MIAO-MB implementation.

### Activities

**4.1 KPI Analysis**
- Collect KPI data
- Analyze trends
- Compare to targets
- Identify improvement areas

**4.2 Internal Audit**
- Conduct comprehensive internal audit
- Assess compliance with procedures
- Verify documentation completeness
- Document findings

**4.3 Customer Satisfaction**
- Conduct customer survey
- Collect feedback
- Analyze satisfaction levels
- Identify improvement opportunities

**4.4 Process Effectiveness**
- Review maintenance effectiveness
- Evaluate procedure usability
- Assess resource adequacy
- Document recommendations

**4.5 Management Review**
- Present audit findings
- Present KPI results
- Discuss customer feedback
- Make improvement decisions

### Deliverables
- KPI analysis report
- Internal audit report
- Customer satisfaction results
- Management review minutes
- Improvement recommendations

---

## Phase 5: Continuous Improvement (Ongoing)

### Objective
Sustain and continuously improve the MIAO-MB system.

### Activities

**5.1 Regular Monitoring**
- Monitor KPIs monthly
- Track maintenance performance
- Identify trends
- Report to management

**5.2 Audit Program**
- Conduct internal audits quarterly
- Focus on high-risk areas
- Document findings
- Track corrective actions

**5.3 CAPA Process**
- Identify non-conformities
- Perform root cause analysis
- Implement corrective actions
- Verify effectiveness

**5.4 Procedure Updates**
- Review procedures annually
- Incorporate lessons learned
- Update based on equipment changes
- Control document versions

**5.5 Risk Assessment Review**
- Review risk assessments annually
- Update based on field data
- Reassess critical equipment
- Document changes

**5.6 Annual Management Review**
- Review all systems
- Assess resource adequacy
- Review improvement progress
- Set objectives for next year

### Ongoing Deliverables
- Monthly KPI reports
- Quarterly audit reports
- CAPA tracking log
- Annual management review
- Updated risk assessments

---

## Success Factors

1. **Commitment:** Management support and resources
2. **Communication:** Clear and transparent messaging
3. **Training:** Adequate staff preparation
4. **Data Management:** Accurate and timely data
5. **Flexibility:** Willingness to adapt
6. **Persistence:** Commitment to continuous improvement
7. **Accountability:** Clear roles and responsibilities
8. **Resources:** Adequate budget and personnel

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

- ❌ Insufficient initial planning
- ❌ Lack of staff training
- ❌ Incomplete documentation
- ❌ Inconsistent procedure application
- ❌ Poor data management
- ❌ Insufficient management engagement
- ❌ Lack of follow-up on findings
- ❌ Inadequate resource allocation

## Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation Strategy |
|------|-------------------|
| Staff resistance | Early engagement, clear communication |
| Inadequate resources | Executive sponsorship, budget planning |
| Incomplete data | Training, standardized forms |
| Lack of sustainability | Management review, incentives |
| System not used | Ongoing training, accessibility |
| Loss of momentum | Regular reviews, visible progress |
